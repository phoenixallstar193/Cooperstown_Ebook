The Cooperstown Experience

What to expect from the greatest week of your child's baseball life

Cooperstown Dreams Park (CDP) is often called the Disney World of baseball. Since 1996, teams from across the country have traveled to Milford, New York for a week-long tournament that players remember for the rest of their lives. From Memorial Day to Labor Day, up to 104 teams compete each week — and every single one leaves changed.

Important: This guide covers Cooperstown Dreams Park (CDP) only — not Cooperstown All Star Village. These are two separate facilities. Make sure you know which one your team is registered for before you plan anything.

Who Can Attend?

CDP is reserved for 12-year-old players. Players must not turn 13 before May 1st of the tournament year. Both boys and girls play baseball at CDP each summer.

What's Included in Registration?

Team registration is a minimum of $16,835 (for 11 players and 2 coaches), and each additional player or coach is $1,295. Each player receives:

👕
Uniforms

Home & Away jerseys, 2 pairs of socks, game hat, souvenir hat

🧥
Warm-Up Gear

Batting jacket and long-sleeve shirt

⚾
Trading Cards

4 personalized baseball trading cards

💍
HOF Ring

American Youth Baseball Hall of Fame induction ring

🏠
Housing

7 days / 6 nights on-site in barracks

🍽️
Meals & Laundry

3 meals per day plus daily uniform laundry service

Parent Expectations

The focus is on having fun, not wins. Don't be discouraged if your team doesn't win every game — teams of all skill levels attend each week. Your players will be just as focused on trading pins and playing wiffle ball as on the actual games. Relax and let them soak it all in.

💡 Tip

College-aged young men regularly reminisce about their Cooperstown trip as one of the greatest weeks of their lives. They don't remember the scores — but they remember wiffle ball games, pins, and friendships made in the barracks.

Getting There

Travel tips, directions & shipping logistics

Driving vs. Flying

Many families choose to drive, especially those within 10–12 hours. If you haven't seen the northeastern US, driving is highly recommended — the scenery is beautiful and you can stop at NYC, Hershey PA, or Niagara Falls along the way.

If flying, the two closest major airports are Albany (~1.5 hours) and Syracuse (~1.75 hours). If you fly, pack your player's glove in their carry-on — bats must be checked.

RV Warning: CDP does NOT allow RVs to enter the facility, and you cannot walk in from outside. If traveling by RV, you'll need to find alternate transportation each day.

Preparation

Weather

Upstate NY is famous for its unpredictable weather — pack accordingly

⚠ Pack for ALL Seasons. Seriously.

Upstate NY can be cool and brisk in the early weeks and brutally hot in late July and August. One family experienced 40°F nights in late June — and then 90°F days two days later. You could literally go from jackets and blankets to tank tops and flip flops within 24 hours.

☀️ Hot Weather

Sunscreen, cooling towels, refillable water bottles. Concession stands sell water and Gatorade.

🌧️ Rain & Cold

Rain ponchos, hoodie, light jacket. It WILL rain at some point. The concession stands sell hot chocolate and coffee!

If you forget something, local shops in Cooperstown and Oneonta have you covered (see Shopping section). Many local shops sell hoodies and tank tops year-round.

The Culture

Trading Pins

The heartbeat of the Cooperstown experience — don't skimp on this one

Don't skimp on the pins — go all out. It is 100% worth it.

Pin trading has become THE biggest part of the experience beside the games. Players start trading the moment they check in and don't stop all week.

How Many to Order

Most teams bring 100–125 pins per player, depending on how many teams attend your week. Order early — many companies offer big discounts for advance orders. A smart strategy is 100 regular pins + 25 limited edition pins. Limited edition pins are the most sought-after.

Order pins for siblings too — they enjoy trading just as much as the players!

Pin Tips

  • CDP requests all pins be removed from individual plastic bags before arriving — prevents bags from blowing everywhere in the village.
  • The most popular display option is the pin trading bag sold at the Retail Center for just under $30 — much sturdier than a towel.
  • Trade pins with the umpires too — umpire pins are some of the most popular!
  • Take pins when going into Cooperstown town — stores and restaurants participate in pin trading too!
  • Paint or mark your wiffle balls so you know which ones are yours.

Certified birth certificate with raised seal. Copies are NOT accepted. Valid passports and state IDs are accepted. Will be returned at checkout.

Agreement to Participate / Liability Waiver

Two-page liability release. Player, parent, AND coach must all sign.

The Grounds

The Facility

CDP spans 165 acres of the most beautiful baseball grounds in America

CDP spreads across 165 immaculately maintained acres. Everything is decorated in red, white and blue, and the facility is obsessively clean. The grounds crew is extraordinary — even after heavy rain the fields look great.

Two Distinct Areas

⚾ Baseball Village

Players, coaches & umpires ONLY (except Day 1 during check-in). Contains: Barracks, Bath Houses, Batting Cages, Dining Pavilion, Infirmary, Laundry, Photo Studio, Operations, Registration & Storehouse.

🏟️ The Park

Open to ALL family members. Contains: All 22 baseball fields, parking lots, concession stands, restrooms, Retail Center, Photo Center, Engraving Center & Family Pavilion.

⚠ Parents are ONLY allowed in the Baseball Village on Day 1 from 2:00–8:00 PM. Parents are also NOT allowed in on checkout day — players pack themselves.

The Fields

There are 22 enclosed grass fields. Each field has lights for night games (rain delays often push games late into the night). Field sizes: 50' pitching distance, 70' base paths, 200' outfield with 8' high fences. Little Majors Stadium (the big one with bleachers) is used for Opening & Closing Ceremonies and the Championship Game.

⚾ Pitching Mound Note

There is not an actual mound — it's a slightly elevated dirt area. Have your pitchers practice on flat ground before arriving so they're prepared.

Home Base

The Barracks

Where the real memories are made — bonds formed, wiffle balls thrown, pins traded

There are 120 barracks within 60 buildings. Each building has an A side and B side, named after famous baseball players (e.g., "16A Whitey Ford"). You won't find out your barrack number until check-in day.

What to Expect

🛏️
Bunk Beds

Twin-size bunk beds (8–10 sets per room). A fitted sheet is provided but it's hard — most bring their own sheets.

📦
Footlocker

37"×23"×21" footlocker per bunk. Pack in a duffle bag — it stores under the lower bunk. Suitcases are hard to store.

❄
A/C

Air conditioning, but bring Box fans and clip-on fans are essential, especially in July/August.

🔒
No Door Locks

NY camp codes don't allow door locks. Bring 2 padlocks for the footlocker. Theft (especially of pins) does occur.

💡
Limited Outlets

Very few outlets. Power strips and extension cords are critical. Zip-tie to the bunk frame.

🌙
Curfew: 10:30 PM

Baseball Operations checks each night. Coaches keep the outside light on until they check — then it's turned off.

Barrack Setup Tips

  • Pack sets of clothing in gallon Ziploc bags — keeps the footlocker organized and clothes dry for showering.
  • Post the game schedule, field number, and uniform color on the wall with painter's tape. Eliminates constant questions.
  • Post the dining menu too — coaches love this!
  • Coaches can take the mattress off an empty top bunk and stack them for better support, then use the top frame for storage.
  • A large King-size flat sheet hung from the top bunk creates privacy and blocks the bright EXIT sign light.
  • Use empty bunks for storage — space is very limited.

Bath Houses

There are NO bathrooms in the barracks. Players shower in separate communal bath houses (players separate from coaches). Coaches escort players there and wait outside.

  • Players MUST wear swim trunks and Crocs/slides — no bare feet allowed.
  • To conserve water, showers auto-shut-off. Some bring a spring clamp to hold the button!
  • Bring 2 towels — use one while the other is being washed. Microfiber dries faster.
  • Mesh shower caddy is essential for carrying toiletries to the bath house.

Laundry

CDP washes ALL clothing at no charge. At registration, the coach receives a large laundry bag with numbered mesh bags inside. All white knickers go in the team bag. Each player gets an individual numbered mesh bag for personal clothing. Laundry drop-off/pick-up is at the Dining Pavilion. Coach fills out a form each time.

💡 Laundry Tip

Label everything with name AND barrack number. Make sure socks are not rolled up — the laundry service doesn't open the mesh bags. Label each individual sock!

The Week

Weekly Itinerary — Day by Day

From the moment your player checks in to the final goodbye — everything you need to know

The CDP tournament week runs 7 days — arrival on Day 1, first games on Day 2, pool play through Day 4, single elimination starting Day 5, and the Championship Game on Day 6 night. Day 7 is checkout morning. Game schedules are published approximately 3 days before your week begins on the CDP website and mobile app.

⚾ All times are subject to change and weather dependent.

Rain is very common in upstate NY. CDP does a great job communicating delays via the mobile app and text alerts. Games are sometimes played late into the night to make up rain delays — be prepared for anything!

What to Expect All Week

🌧️ Rain Delays

It WILL rain at some point. CDP pushes games late into the night to get them in. Download the CDP app and sign up for text alerts (text "baseball" to 25827). Concession stands sell hot chocolate and coffee for cold evenings.

📌 Pin Trading

Players trade pins at their barrack tables, at the Pin Zone, at the Family Pavilion, and anywhere they go. Dedicated pin trading events run multiple times daily. Umpire pins are the most sought-after. Trade with restaurants in Cooperstown village too!

🥎 Wiffle Ball

Arguably as big as the real games. Multiple games run simultaneously between barracks at all hours. Players play in the rain, before breakfast, after curfew if they can get away with it. Paint your wiffle balls so you know which are yours.

📱 Cell Phones

Players are allowed phones in the barracks — coach's discretion on hours. Most coaches implement a 10:00 PM phone curfew. Cell coverage is good for all major carriers. No Wi-Fi is available in the facility.

🔒 Barrack Security

No locks on barrack doors (NY camp codes). Theft occurs — especially trading pins during games. Use padlocks on footlockers for valuables. Keep expensive items locked up when the team is playing.

🌡️ Weather Swings

Upstate NY in summer: expect anything from 40°F nights in early weeks to 90°F+ days in late July/August. You may go from jackets and blankets to tank tops in 24 hours. Pack for all seasons — seriously.

⚾ Parent Viewing

All fields are fully enclosed with covered viewing areas. Plastic chairs are provided — no need to bring your own. Pop-up tents are NOT allowed. Umbrellas are fine. An electrical outlet in each viewing area lets you charge devices.

🏃 Between Games

Players have significant downtime between games. The Batting Cages (22 cages inside the Baseball Village) are first-come first-served — go early before games. Card games, team trivia, dance competitions, and movie projectors on the barrack wall are popular rainy-day activities.

🧺 Laundry

CDP washes ALL clothing at no charge. White knickers go in the team bag. Personal clothing goes in individual numbered mesh bags. Coaches fill out a laundry form each time. Drop-off and pick-up at the Dining Pavilion.

⏱️ How Fast It Goes

Every parent who has been to Cooperstown will tell you the same thing: it goes by impossibly fast. All the anticipation leading up to your week, and before you know it you're packing up on Day 7 morning. Soak in every single moment. Watch every game, stay for the fireworks, let your player trade every pin they can. The week is priceless — and it will be over before you're ready for it to be.

On the Field

Game Rules & Info

CDP follows MLB rules with specific modifications coaches should know

Game Format

⚾
6 Innings

All games are 6 innings with NO time limits.

🌧️
Rain Rule

Game called if 4 innings are complete.

💥
Mercy Rule

12 runs after 4 complete innings.

🕰️
Be On Time

Coaches and players at field minimum 15 minutes before start.

🏟️
200' Outfield

Short fences = LOTS of home runs. Cooperstown is known for them!

📺
Livestream

$1.99/week for app access to watch games. Don't purchase before arriving — week starts when you buy.

Key Rule Modifications

  • Extra Hitters (EH): 0, 1, 2 or 3 EHs at coach's discretion — must bat in 10th, 11th, 12th position. No DH.
  • Re-entry: Starters may re-enter once in same offensive spot. Substitutes cannot re-enter.
  • Courtesy Runner: Allowed for catcher and pitcher at any time. Not a substitution.
  • Bunting: Fake bunt + full swing = batter is out, dead ball, no runners advance.
  • Sliding: Must slide or avoid contact on close plays. No head-first slide into home plate.
  • No Metal Cleats — molded cleats only.
  • Jerseys must be tucked in at all times. No jewelry of any kind.
  • Red hat is a SOUVENIR — only the navy hat can be worn. Violating this risks a 2-game suspension.
  • Seeds & gum are NOT allowed on CDP property. Don't include them in swag bags either.

Skills Competitions (Day 2)

Field 1
Road Runner

One player timed running the bases home to home. 5-second penalty per missed base.

Field 2
Golden Arm

3 throws from centerfield (~125 ft) to home plate. Scored on accuracy and distance.

Fields 7 & 10
King of Swat

5 swings off a JUGGS® pitching machine. Most home runs wins. CDP provides the bat.

Field 3
Around the Horn

8–9 players compete as a team. Fastest time wins. Each base must be tagged.

💡 Tip

Coaches should decide which 3 players compete in individual skills competitions before arrival. The rest of the team competes in Around the Horn.

Requirements

Umpires

Every team must provide an umpire — here's what you need to know

Your umpire enters a pool

Your umpire will NOT be assigned to your games. CDP puts all umpires into a random pool to prevent favoritism. Umpires share barracks together for the entire week.

NEVER pay an umpire in advance

Fees range from $800–$1,300 for the week, or some teams cover travel expenses and they do it free. Work this out with your coach/organization.

💡 Tip

Local umpires can be hired if you're unable to secure your own — just make sure they're approved by CDP. Many umpires are in the Cooperstown Facebook groups. Umpires often have their own trading pins and they can be the most sought-after pins of the week!

Quick Answers

FAQs

The most common questions from first-time Cooperstown families — answered

⚾ Is there a curfew?
Yes — 10:30 PM every night. Baseball Operations checks each barrack nightly. Many coaches also implement a 10:00 PM phone curfew.
⚾ Is Wi-Fi available at CDP?
No. Wi-Fi is NOT available in the facility. Cell service is good for all major carriers, but if your player has a Wi-Fi-only phone, plan accordingly.
⚾ Do players have to wait for their coach before entering the village?
No — players and parents can go ahead and start unpacking the barrack. The coach only needs to be present for team Registration.
⚾ Can players have cell phones in the barracks?
Yes, though it's ultimately the coach's discretion. Many coaches implement phone policies to help with sleep and focus.
⚾ Do we have to stay until after the championship game?
No — you can leave once your team is eliminated. The earliest this happens is Day 5. Most hotels book for the full week regardless, so staying for the whole thing is highly recommended.
⚾ Are trading pins required?
Not required, but they have become one of the absolute best parts of the trip. If you're looking to save money somewhere, look elsewhere — don't skimp on pins.
⚾ Do I need to bring my own chairs?
No — plastic outdoor chairs are provided in the viewing area of each field. Note: pop-up tents are NOT allowed anywhere in the facility. Umbrellas are permitted for shade.
⚾ Is there a Lost & Found?
Yes — located at Guest Services at the South Entrance of the Baseball Village.
⚾ What is prohibited at CDP?
AlcoholPop-Up TentsDrones Animals (except service animals)RVs Seeds & GumMetal CleatsWood/Altered Bats
⚾ When will we get our game schedules?
Typically 3 days before Day 1 of your week. Available on the CDP website and mobile app. Call CDP a few months in advance if you want a ballpark number of teams (for pin ordering purposes).
⚾ Can arms sleeves be worn?
Yes — for all players except pitchers, who may not wear white or patterned arm sleeves.
⚾ Remember...Have Fun
The absolute best thing to remember at Cooperstown Dreams Park is to soak in every moment and have fun. It is a once-in-a-lifetime experience where kids make lifelong friends, bond with teammates, all while playing a game that they love.

It's not just about baseball. It's about friendships — the ones that are enhanced and the new ones made. It's about teamwork. It's about memories. It's about life lessons. If your player has the opportunity to go, they definitely should. I promise you will not regret it.
